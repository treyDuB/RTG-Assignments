My Code

Loading light objects A3-load

I added every attribute from the "LIGHT" directly to a struct in RTG. I decided to leave Tint and power seperate becasue sometimes power is a function of the distance.

Adding Direct Lighting to Materials A3-materials

To pass Lighting information to the objects I repurposed World and made a struct Light. In that struct I packaged everything into 4 vec4s as Tint + Strength or Power, Position of the light in world + Angle or Fov, Radius + Limit + Blend + Type of Light, and the direction that light points. This packaging made all of the information align nicely as attributes in the vertex storage buffer. I essentially passed in an array of Lights to the object.frag where I then looped throught the lights in the scene. For each type of light I added the proper light energy * attenuation to the energy at that point.
